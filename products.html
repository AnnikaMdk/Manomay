Run Your Halls.

The command centre for owners, managers and agents. Full control over listings, pricing, calendars, and every booking request that comes in.

  • Set half-day, full-day, and custom slot rates
  • Visual calendar to manage availability at a glance
  • Approve or reject customer and agent booking requests
  • Confirm bookings after verifying advance payment
  • Revenue, occupancy & performance reports
  • Link service providers directly to your halls
