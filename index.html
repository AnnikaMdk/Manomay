About Manomay

We Build Software
for Every Corner
of an Event

Manomay is an Indian event technology company building a comprehensive platform that connects every person involved in making an event happen — from the owner who listed the hall to the customer celebrating their special day.

Our platform supports six distinct stakeholder roles, each with tailored tools, workflows, and permissions — so everyone from administrators to agents has exactly what they need and nothing they don't.

Headquartered in Karnataka, we're building for India — with multi-language ambitions and deep respect for how events are run here.

See Our Products

Owner

High-level control — add halls, manage bookings, set pricing, approve requests, generate revenue reports.

Venue Manager

Manages assigned halls — approves bookings, generates occupancy reports, handles day-to-day operations.

Service Provider

Links services to halls, manages daily bookings, approves requests, tracks service demand via reports.

Agent

Assists owners with hall additions and bookings, tracks all initiated booking activities and pending approvals.

Customer

Books halls and services, tracks approvals step by step, gets notified, shares bookings, views their event calendar.

Admin

Platform-wide authority — manages all users, venues, approvals, and comprehensive cross-platform analytics.

Features

Everything You Need,
Nothing You Don't

Built ground-up for India's event ecosystem — every feature exists because a real venue or customer needed it.

Smart Calendar

Visual booking calendar for owners and customers — instantly see available dates, confirmed events, and pending requests side by side.

Multi-step Booking Flow

Transparent status pipeline — Requested → Approved → Advance Paid → Confirmed. Customers always know exactly where they stand.

Live Notifications

Push alerts at every milestone — requests, approvals, confirmations, payment receipts, and cancellations delivered instantly.

Role-based Reports

Tailored analytics per role — owners track revenue, managers see occupancy, service providers analyse demand. Everyone sees what matters to them.

Add-on Services

Catering, decor, AV, photography — service providers link their offerings to halls. Customers book them as add-ons in the same flow.

Flexible Pricing

Half-day, full-day, custom slots — owners set rates their own way. Seasonal pricing, advance requirements, and booking terms all supported.

How It Works

From Browse
to Big Day

The Manomay booking flow is simple, transparent, and built around your confidence at every step.

1

Discover Venues

Browse halls nearby, filter by city, capacity, date, and budget. See real photos, timings, and available services.

2

Send a Request

Submit a booking request for your chosen date and slot. Add services if you need them. The owner reviews it.

3

Get Approved

You get notified the moment you're approved. Pay the advance to lock your booking and move to confirmed.

4

Celebrate!

Your event is locked in. Get reminders, share details with guests, and walk into your event stress-free.
